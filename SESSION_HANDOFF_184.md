## Findings that shape the next sessions

1. **Gate reconciliation at open.** `pipeline_manifest.py`'s `GUARDED` list stopped at
   `SESSION_HANDOFF_182.md`; `SESSION_HANDOFF_183.md` was present on disk but missing from the list,
   and `pipeline_manifest.json`'s stored hash for `pipeline_manifest.py` didn't match the file's
   actual content. Same failure shape as the one D239 fixed at S156 — a manifest `--write` that ran
   before the file reached its final edited state. Fixed (183 added to `GUARDED`, manifest
   reissued); worth a standing note if it recurs a third time.
2. **The SM-family chapter model is two tiers, not one.** Six chapters (Ultramarines, Iron Hands,
   Salamanders, Imperial Fists, Raven Guard, White Scars) have no dedicated MFM — their units live
   inside `MFM_Space_Marines.txt`, and D42's union-plus-override model is correct for them as
   written. Five chapters (Black Templars, Blood Angels, Dark Angels, Deathwatch, Space Wolves) ship
   their own complete MFM file and never reference the generic one. Confirmed directly against the
   Black Templars file: 76 units total, self-contained, no reliance on
   `MFM_Space_Marines.txt`.
3. **`resolveUnits()` doesn't know the difference.** It unions the full generic Adeptus Astartes pool
   into every `is_subfaction` chapter unconditionally. For Black Templars this leaks 90 units its own
   MFM excludes — every Librarian variant (Black Templars take no Psykers) and 11 other-chapter named
   characters (Tigurius, Vulkan He'stan, Shrike, Kor'sarro Khan, and others). These are currently
   selectable in list building for all five Tier-2 chapters — a live D0 violation, not a cosmetic gap.
4. **B90 opened and scoped as three strictly-typed turns** (engine flag, data rebuild, assertion) —
   see the ticket body for the full acceptance criteria. It now sits ahead of B87/B88/B89 in
   practice, though the MFM v1.1 refresh arc tickets are untouched and still valid; Ryan asked that
   B90 land before any further faction work, not that the v1.1 arc be abandoned.
