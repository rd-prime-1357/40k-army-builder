## What shipped

`rules_assertions.py`. Two new private methods on `Sources` — `_apply_chapter_point_overrides()`
and `_apply_chapter_keyword_additions()` — are direct mirrors of `index.html`'s
`applyChapterPointOverrides()` (B56d) and `applyChapterKeywordAdditions()` (B132), including the
non-mutation rule each enforces: `self.units()` caches parsed JSON and hands the same nested dict
objects to every pool built from it, so a shallow `dict(u)` copy alone is not enough for the
keyword-additions case — each touched `model_groups` entry needs its own copy too. `resolved_pool()`
now calls both, gated on the army being a chapter subfaction, immediately after the generic+chapter
union.

Verified directly, not just through the gate: all 5 generic-pool Dark Angels Characters that should
carry Deathwing now do (Captain/Chaplain/Librarian In Terminator Armour, Ancient In Terminator
Armour, Bladeguard Ancient); no leakage into `resolved_pool('Ultramarines')` or the raw generic
`Adeptus Astartes` block; idempotent across repeat calls with no duplicate keyword; an untouched
unit is the *same object* (`is`, not `==`) across the resolved pool and the generic block; point
overrides produce a real price difference where one is scoped (Blood Angels' Bladeguard Veteran
Squad prices 85/95 against generic 80/90). Negative-tested by reverting the new keyword-additions
method to a no-op: the gate fails and names exactly the 6 Deathwing-family records, confirming the
gate's pass depends on this change rather than being incidental.

B131's 6 placeholder `EXEMPT` entries are removed. The zero-bearer gate's named-exemption count
moves **36 → 30**, exactly as scoped; all 30 remaining exemptions (24 Vehicle/B128, 4 Marks/B126,
1 Spawn, 1 Harlequins) resolve exactly as before — confirmed by re-running the gate rather than
assumed. `b129_zero_bearer_gate`'s docstring and the shorter `B129` assertion-registration
description are both rewritten to describe the current mechanism instead of the B131 transitional
state.

A documented, currently-latent gap: the mirror does not read `roster_mode`, so a future
`'complete'`-mode chapter subfaction would need a matching branch before `resolved_pool()` could be
trusted for it. No such subfaction exists in `faction_taxonomy.json` today. Called out explicitly
in the function's own docstring rather than left for rediscovery, matching how the missing
`chapter_point_overrides` application was flagged and then closed in this same session.
